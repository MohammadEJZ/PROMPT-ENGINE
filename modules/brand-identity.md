# Specialist module: Brand identity and visual systems

Use for naming, positioning, logos, visual identity, brand guidelines, and product branding.

## Discover
- Brand name, category, audience, market, and competitors
- Purpose, promise, positioning, and differentiation
- Desired attributes and personality
- Cultural, linguistic, legal, and production constraints
- Required assets and usage environments

## Strategy before visuals
Define brand essence, positioning, audience, value proposition, personality, tone of voice, and visual territory before specifying a logo.

## Identity system
Specify logo concept and rationale, symbol/wordmark relationship, typography, color palette with values, graphic language, iconography, imagery, layout principles, and usage rules. Include light/dark and small-scale variants when relevant.

## Rules
Avoid unsupported claims of trademark availability. Ensure concepts are distinctive and practical across digital and physical use. Explain trade-offs and avoid relying on visual effects that fail in monochrome or small sizes.

## Acceptance
The identity is coherent, recognizable, scalable, usable, and supported by clear rationale and practical guidelines.
