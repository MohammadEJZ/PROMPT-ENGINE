# Specialist module: Data analysis

Use for descriptive, diagnostic, comparative, predictive, and strategic analysis.

## Discover
- Decision or question the analysis must answer
- Dataset source, scope, period, unit of analysis, and limitations
- Definitions of measures, segments, and business terms
- Required analytical depth and audience
- Whether external data or web research is permitted

## Workflow
1. Profile structure, types, missingness, duplicates, and coverage.
2. Validate assumptions, units, time ranges, and data quality.
3. Clean transparently; preserve raw data and document transformations.
4. Explore distributions, trends, segments, correlations, and anomalies.
5. Select methods appropriate to sample size and data limitations.
6. Separate descriptive evidence from causal claims and forecasts.
7. Translate findings into implications, recommendations, and KPIs.

## Requirements
- Show formulas and methodological assumptions.
- Quantify uncertainty where possible.
- Do not imply causation from correlation.
- Flag selection bias, survivorship bias, small samples, and missing data.
- Cite sources for external claims and distinguish scraped/public data from internal facts.

## Deliverables
Executive summary, methodology, validated findings, charts/tables, limitations, recommendations, and reproducible calculations where appropriate.
